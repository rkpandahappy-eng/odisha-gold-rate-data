Privacy Policy – ଓଡ଼ିଶା ସୁନା ଦର (Odisha Gold Rate)

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What the app collects

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, phone number, email or location. Your language and city choices are stored only on your phone.

Internet use

The app downloads public rate files from this website (GitHub Pages). GitHub may log standard technical data such as IP address for these requests, under GitHub's own privacy policy.

Calculator

Numbers you type in the bill calculator stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere. Sharing an estimate only happens when you tap Share.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and basic device and app-use information. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for it with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Sponsored card

Some cities may show a card from a local jeweller, marked "Sponsored". Tapping it opens WhatsApp; the app itself sends nothing to the jeweller.

Morning alert

If you turn on the daily morning alert, the app checks today's rates in the background and shows a notification on your phone. This uses the same public rate files and sends no personal data. You can turn it off any time in Settings.

Disclaimer

Rates are indicative and for information only. Showroom prices vary with making charges, GST and local rates. This is not financial advice.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the contact details on the app's Google Play listing.
